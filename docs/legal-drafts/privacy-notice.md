# Privacy notice for website visitors (DRAFT, not reviewed by a lawyer)

For the website of [business name] ("the business"). This notice is about the
request form on this website.

## Who is responsible

[Business name, address, contact] decides why and how your request is used.
[Your legal name] runs the form for the business and stores the requests on
its behalf.

## What we store when you send the form

- Your name, phone number and, if you give it, your email address.
- What you asked for: the service, the type of property, the ZIP code, the
  age of the roof, when you want to start, whether it is an insurance claim,
  and your note.
- The time of the request.
- A scrambled form of your internet address, used only to stop abuse. We
  cannot turn it back into your address.

We do not use cookies for advertising and we do not sell your data.

## What it is used for

- To let the business contact you about your request.
- To send you one confirmation email, if you gave an email address.
- After the job, the business may send you one email asking for a review.

## Who sees it

- The business: the people whose Telegram chats and email address are
  connected to this form.
- [Your legal name], as the operator of the form. The operator's own screens
  show only the type of service, the status and the age of a request, not
  your name, phone, email or note.
- The companies that carry and store the data for us: [database provider and
  region], [hosting provider], [email provider], Telegram.

## If the business's service is paused

Your request is still saved. The business may not see it until its service is
active again, so calling the business is faster. In that case you get no
confirmation email.

## How long it is kept

- As long as the business uses the service, unless the business or you ask
  for deletion earlier.
- After the business stops using the service: up to 60 days, then it may be
  deleted by hand. There is no automatic purge. **[REVIEW FLAG]** The owner and
  lawyer must decide whether this is a promise or a permission.

## Your rights

You can ask the business to show you, correct or delete your request.
