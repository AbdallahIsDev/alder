| 10 days after the due date | We warn you that the service will be terminated. |

address. A notice can arrive up to one day after the moment in the table,
never before it, except a reminder or a warning, which can arrive up to one
hour early.

## 4. What "suspended" means

- Your Telegram chats show your service status and nothing else.
- Your website form still accepts requests. We store them. You do not see
  them while the service is suspended. We tell you that a request arrived,
  without its details.
- A visitor who sends the form is told that the request was saved and that
  calling you is faster. The visitor gets no confirmation email.
- When you pay, the service is switched on again and you see every request
  that arrived meanwhile.

- Your data is not deleted at termination. See section 6.
- If you pay within the retention period, we switch the service on again
